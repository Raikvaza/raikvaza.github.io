Privacy Policy

Watch faces by Akhmetzhan Seitkhan · Effective 16 November 2026

My watch faces collect no personal data and send nothing anywhere. They contain no code, request no permissions and have no internet access.

The time, date and complications come from Wear OS and the apps you pick for them. The watch face only shows them, and Wear OS keeps your settings on your watch.

Purchases

Google Play handles payment, and I never see your card details. For each order, Google gives me the order number, price, taxes, device model and your country (and, where tax rules require it, your region and postal code). I use them only for accounting, taxes, refunds and support.
