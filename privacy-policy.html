Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

This policy covers the Wear OS watch faces that Akhmetzhan Seitkhan publishes on Google Play, whose package names start with com.raiko.dials. The first is Adrift (com.raiko.dials.adrift).

In short

Our watch faces collect no personal data, and they send nothing anywhere.

What our watch faces are

Each watch face is built with Google's Watch Face Format. It contains only images, fonts and a description of the layout. It contains no program code, requests no Android permissions and has no internet access. Wear OS draws it on your watch.

Data we collect

None. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information shown on the watch face

The time, the date and the complications you choose (for example battery, steps, unread notifications or your next event) are supplied by Wear OS and by the apps that provide those complications. The watch face only displays this information on your watch. It doesn't store or transmit it. Complication providers handle their data under their own privacy policies.

Your settings

The choices you make in the watch face editor (for example the planet, the style, the animation and the complications) are saved by Wear OS on your watch.

Google Play

When you install a watch face from Google Play, Google processes data under the Google Privacy Policy. Google gives developers aggregated statistics, such as install counts, ratings and reviews, that don't identify you. Reviews you write are public on Google Play, and we may reply to them there.

If you buy a watch face, Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or bank details. For each order, Google gives us its number, date, price and taxes, the device model, and the buyer's country (and, where tax rules need it, their city, state and postal code). We use these only for accounting, taxes, refunds and support.

If you contact us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and we keep them only as long as needed to help you. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Children

Our watch faces collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
